MIRASH Research Calculator v5.0.0

MIRASH (Metabolic–Inflammatory–Renal–Age–Sex–Hematologic) is a laboratory-derived muscle-health/sarcopenia score based on age, sex, and five routine laboratory measures. The score was developed in CHARLS 2011, temporally evaluated in CHARLS 2015, and independently externally validated in NHANES 1999–2002.

Research-use notice. This calculator reports only the MIRASH score and the model-estimated sarcopenia probability. It does not estimate an individual's handgrip strength, skeletal muscle index, or the probability of other muscle phenotypes. MIRASH is not a diagnostic device and does not replace direct assessment of muscle strength, muscle quantity, physical performance, or clinician judgment. No diagnostic or treatment cutoff is proposed.
Phenotype framework. In the study, MIRASH was evaluated against six additional muscle-health phenotypes at the cohort level: handgrip strength, skeletal muscle index (SMI), low muscle strength, low estimated muscle mass, possible sarcopenia, and severe sarcopenia. These analyses are used to characterize which dimensions of muscle health are captured by MIRASH; they are not individual-level calculator outputs.

Inputs

Triglycerides and CRP must be greater than 0 because the MIRASH equation uses their natural logarithms. Uric acid may be entered in mg/dL or µmol/L; values entered in µmol/L are automatically converted to mg/dL using 1 mg/dL = 59.48 µmol/L before calculation.

Model specification

MIRASH core equation

Score = -5.487398507960009 + 0.12326834468595758×age - 0.1899312810882511×sex_female - 0.1753188540347149×hemoglobin + 0.02671598208556792×HDL - 0.5530624730258957×ln(triglycerides) - 0.1779895176025112×uric_acid_mg_dL - 0.11104092546219071×ln(CRP)

P(sarcopenia) = 1 / [1 + exp(-Score)]

Female sex = 1 and male sex = 0. Units: age in years; hemoglobin in g/dL; HDL-C and triglycerides in mg/dL; uric acid is converted to mg/dL before calculation; CRP in mg/L. Natural logarithms are applied only to triglycerides and CRP. No z-score standardization is used.

Why the other six phenotypes are not displayed as individual predictions

The additional phenotype analyses were designed to evaluate what dimensions of muscle health are captured by the MIRASH score at the cohort level. They were not developed as six separate clinical prediction models for patient-level deployment. In particular, mapped handgrip-strength and SMI values can inherit the direction of the common MIRASH score and may extrapolate implausibly when individual predictors are moved to extreme values. They are therefore retained for research evaluation rather than presented as calculator outputs.

Scope and limitations

MIRASH was evaluated in adults aged 60 years or older. NHANES serves as an independent external validation dataset, although some muscle phenotypes use measurement systems that differ from CHARLS. Those measurement-different analyses are interpreted as supportive external construct validation. The calculator is intended for research use and does not establish a diagnostic or treatment threshold.

All calculations are performed locally in the browser; this static page does not transmit or store entered values. Version 5.0.0 implements the MIRASH core score and primary sarcopenia probability only. Internal self-test pending.
