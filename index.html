MIRASH Muscle Health Research Calculator v4.0.0

MIRASH (Metabolic–Inflammatory–Renal–Age–Sex–Hematologic) is a parsimonious research score based on age, sex, and five routine laboratory measures. It was developed in CHARLS 2011, temporally evaluated in CHARLS 2015, and independently replicated at the construct level in NHANES 1999–2002.

Research-use notice. MIRASH is intended for research screening and risk enrichment. It is not a diagnostic device and does not replace direct assessment of muscle strength, muscle quantity, physical performance, or clinician judgment. No diagnostic or treatment cutoff is proposed.
How to interpret the seven outputs. The sarcopenia probability is the primary MIRASH output. Low muscle mass, severe sarcopenia, and low muscle strength are secondary research probabilities; possible sarcopenia is exploratory. Handgrip strength and SMI are continuous research estimates rather than probabilities and should not be treated as substitutes for direct measurement.

Inputs

Enter values in the units shown above. The calculator does not automatically convert mmol/L, µmol/L, or other units. Triglycerides and CRP must be greater than 0 because the model uses their natural logarithms.

Model specification

Compact MIRASH core equation

LP = -5.487398507960009 + 0.12326834468595758×age - 0.1899312810882511×sex_female - 0.1753188540347149×hemoglobin + 0.02671598208556792×HDL - 0.5530624730258957×ln(triglycerides) - 0.1779895176025112×uric_acid - 0.11104092546219071×ln(CRP)

Female sex = 1 and male sex = 0. Units: age in years; hemoglobin in g/dL; HDL-C, triglycerides, and uric acid in mg/dL; CRP in mg/L. Natural logarithms are applied only to triglycerides and CRP. No z-score standardization is used.

P(sarcopenia) = logistic(LP)

Scope and limitations

The current MIRASH model was evaluated in adults aged 60 years or older. CHARLS and NHANES do not provide identical measurements for every phenotype; NHANES strength evidence is proxy-based and DXA ASMI serves as an independent muscle-mass construct replication. The calculator therefore supports research interpretation rather than clinical diagnosis.

All calculations are performed locally in the browser; this static page does not transmit or store entered values. Version 4.0.0 uses the final compact MIRASH specification dated 18 September 2026. No diagnostic or treatment cutoff is optimized or recommended. Internal self-test pending.
