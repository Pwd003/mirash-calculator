MIRASH Sarcopenia Calculator v2.0.0

Frozen clinical laboratory–based research formula for contemporaneous sarcopenia assessment in adults aged ≥60 years
What this calculator does. It applies the fixed MIRASH equation exactly as evaluated in the study. It returns the frozen linear predictor (LP) and the estimated probability for the original frozen sarcopenia target only.
What it does not do. MIRASH does not replace formal measurement of muscle strength, muscle quantity, or physical performance. The seven post-freeze muscle/sarcopenia phenotypes were used to examine the construct spectrum; this equation does not provide calibrated probabilities for those other phenotypes. No universal diagnostic or treatment cutoff was established.
Target population: age ≥60 years.

Frozen equation and transformations

Enter raw values in the units shown above. Only creatinine, triglycerides, CRP, glucose, and HbA1c are natural-log transformed. No z-score, mean/SD, or median/IQR standardization is applied.

LP = -3.560992184381921 + 0.1277818820080819 × Age - 0.18995170510177203 × Female - 0.1470669067614975 × Hemoglobin + 0.00244916790473318 × Platelets - 0.37898821247535686 × ln(Creatinine) - 0.00584560049501891 × Total cholesterol + 0.03380228550218713 × HDL-C - 0.3078444088236499 × ln(Triglycerides) - 0.13470542496673812 × Uric acid - 0.10759903465824627 × ln(CRP) - 0.4947046847487329 × ln(Glucose) - 0.924042456215138 × ln(HbA1c) Probability = 1 / [1 + exp(-LP)]

Important: historical internal variable names beginning with z_ were naming artifacts and do not indicate z-score standardization.

Applicability/QC note

The calculator checks whether entered biomarker values fall outside the frozen development-domain 8-IQR QC boundaries. These are not clinical reference intervals, do not define eligibility for treatment, and are not scaling constants. A warning does not block calculation; it flags extrapolation beyond the development-domain QC range.

Privacy and reproducibility

All calculations run locally in your browser. Entered values are not transmitted, uploaded, or stored by this static page.

Version 2.0.0 uses the unchanged frozen MIRASH coefficients. The repository also contains a machine-readable model specification and fixed numerical validation cases.

Research implementation aid for the MIRASH development and multistage evaluation study. Please cite the final peer-reviewed manuscript when available. Clinical deployment requires independent prospective impact evaluation and appropriate local governance.
