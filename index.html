Model equation and units

All logarithms are natural logarithms. Enter raw laboratory values; the calculator performs logarithmic transformations automatically.

LP = -3.560992184381921 + 0.1277818820080819 × Age - 0.18995170510177203 × Female - 0.1470669067614975 × Hemoglobin + 0.00244916790473318 × Platelets - 0.37898821247535686 × ln(Creatinine) - 0.00584560049501891 × Total cholesterol + 0.03380228550218713 × HDL-C - 0.3078444088236499 × ln(Triglycerides) - 0.13470542496673812 × Uric acid - 0.10759903465824627 × ln(CRP) - 0.4947046847487329 × ln(Glucose) - 0.924042456215138 × ln(HbA1c)

Probability = 1 / [1 + exp(-LP)]. Female=1; male=0.

Privacy and versioning

All calculations are performed locally in your browser. This static calculator does not transmit, store, or upload entered values.

Calculator version: 1.0.0. Formula corresponds to the fixed MIRASH model evaluated in CHARLS 2015 and NHANES 1999–2002.

Research implementation aid. Please cite the MIRASH development and validation manuscript when available. Before clinical deployment, local calibration and prospective impact evaluation are recommended.
