MIRASH Research Calculator v7.0.0

MIRASH (Metabolic–Inflammatory–Renal–Age–Sex–Hematologic) is a laboratory-derived muscle-health/sarcopenia score based on age, sex, and five routine laboratory measures. The score was developed in CHARLS 2011, temporally evaluated in CHARLS 2015, and independently externally validated in NHANES 1999–2002.

Research-use notice. This calculator reports the MIRASH score, the model-estimated sarcopenia probability, and a screen-positive/high-risk classification. The development-derived screen-positive threshold is 14.84% (exact analytic cutoff 0.148374094750412; displayed as approximately 15%). A screen-positive result should prompt confirmatory sarcopenia assessment. It is not a stand-alone diagnosis and does not replace direct assessment of muscle strength, muscle quantity, physical performance, or clinician judgment.
Phenotype framework. In the study, MIRASH was evaluated against six additional muscle-health phenotypes at the cohort level: handgrip strength, skeletal muscle index (SMI), low muscle strength, low estimated muscle mass, possible sarcopenia, and severe sarcopenia. These analyses are used to characterize which dimensions of muscle health are captured by MIRASH; they are not individual-level calculator outputs.

Inputs

Choose the unit actually reported by the laboratory. The calculator converts all inputs to the units used in model development before calculation: hemoglobin to g/dL; HDL-C and triglycerides to mg/dL; uric acid to mg/dL; and CRP to mg/L. Triglycerides and CRP must be greater than 0 because the MIRASH equation uses their natural logarithms. Extreme or unusual input combinations may lie outside the range adequately represented in the development cohort and should not be interpreted as clinically reliable solely from this calculator.

Model specification

MIRASH core equation

UA_c = uric_acid_mg_dL - 4.4520001411438

Score = -6.3588292227466905 + 0.1238711005049799×age - 0.2205425806334254×sex_female - 0.1750523404277856×hemoglobin + 0.0266687491667375×HDL - 0.5611718262015551×ln(triglycerides) - 0.2389832281382797×UA_c + 0.0540754147734259×UA_c² - 0.1075228274977055×ln(CRP)

P(sarcopenia) = 1 / [1 + exp(-Score)]

Female sex = 1 and male sex = 0. Units: age in years; hemoglobin in g/dL; HDL-C and triglycerides in mg/dL; uric acid is converted to mg/dL before calculation; CRP in mg/L. Natural logarithms are applied only to triglycerides and CRP. Uric acid enters the model as a centered quadratic term using the frozen CHARLS 2011 center of 4.4520001411438 mg/dL. The model-estimated uric-acid nadir (~6.66 mg/dL) is a shape descriptor, not a clinical target or treatment threshold. No z-score standardization is used.

Why the other six phenotypes are not displayed as individual predictions

The additional phenotype analyses were designed to evaluate what dimensions of muscle health are captured by the MIRASH score at the cohort level. They were not developed as six separate clinical prediction models for patient-level deployment. In particular, mapped handgrip-strength and SMI values can inherit the direction of the common MIRASH score and may extrapolate implausibly when individual predictors are moved to extreme values. They are therefore retained for research evaluation rather than presented as calculator outputs.

Scope and limitations

MIRASH was evaluated in adults aged 60 years or older. NHANES serves as an independent external transportability dataset, although some muscle phenotypes use measurement systems that differ from CHARLS. The frozen 14.84% screen-positive operating point is intended to identify individuals who should proceed to confirmatory sarcopenia assessment; it is not a stand-alone diagnostic or treatment threshold. A result below the cutoff does not exclude sarcopenia when clinical suspicion is present.

All calculations are performed locally in the browser; this static page does not transmit or store entered values. Version 6.0.0 implements the final frozen quadratic-uric-acid MIRASH score and primary sarcopenia probability only. Internal self-test pending.
